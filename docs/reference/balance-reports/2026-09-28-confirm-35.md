# Roadmap 35 confirmation run, 2026-09-28

the session.

**1. A new search on the changed game.** `sim-search` ran at levels 1, 3, 5, and 7
(40 fights a seat order, 24 moves a step, up to 12 steps, 2 rounds). It started from the
fixed typical hands: at most two heals, context cards ranked by their best context. JSON:
[2026-09-28-search-35.json](2026-09-28-search-35.json).

ran twice from one checkout: once as the game is now (after), and once with the `before-35`
variant undoing the changes (before). That variant was checked to reproduce the pre-change
results exactly. JSON: [2026-09-28-confirm-35.json](2026-09-28-confirm-35.json). With 12,000
fights per monster per level, a field average is good to about ±1 point.

## Findings

1. **Levels 3, 5, and 7 are in band for every monster.**
   - The Dragon is at 45%, 52%, and 42% (it was 33%, 47%, and 40%).
   - The Gladiator is at 47%, 39%, and 49% (it was 38%, 35%, and 47%).
   - No monster is above 69% at any level.
2. **The guardrails rose.** Rounds, Curse of Loki, strokes of luck, and turnarounds from 25
   and 50 points behind are all up 4-5%.
3. **The Dragon at level 1 is better, and still below the floor: 16% → 28%.**
   - Most of the gap is one matchup: the Weeping Angel beats it 94%.
   - Swapping the Angel's **Blink** for a Hit takes that to 43%. That one card is worth 52
     points in this matchup: the Dragon's level-1 hand leads with its two Fire Breaths, and
     Blink removes it for the turn it would breathe.
   - No other card on either side moves the matchup more than 12 points.
   - Level 1 is short: fights 4 to 8 at 9 XP a fight.
4. **The Unicorn beating the Dragon 98% at level 7 is a search artifact, not balance.**
   - The searched level-7 Dragon hand held three Take Wings with little to dive into, and
     the Unicorn's four Blasts knock a flier down every time.
   - A plain hand (3 Fire Breath, Scotch, 4 Fight or Flight, a Delayed Hit) beats the Unicorn
     51% and averages about 51% against the other searched hands.
   - The search, even at double the power, still settles in local optima; a card that only
     pays off in a combination (Take Wing then a melee strike) is hard for it.

## What it means for the plan

- Tasks 2 and 3 are confirmed at levels 3-7, and at level 1 for the Gladiator.
- **The Dragon at level 1 is an owner decision.** The choices are:
  1. accept it as the Wizard's fragile start (level 1 lasts about four fights, and the gap is
     one card);
  2. give Blink an answer, not a nerf. The redesigned Unconquerable Horn counters it, but
     the Dragon cannot hold that card, so a Dragon counter or a shared one from the counter
     family ([12](../../roadmap/12-new-content-backlog.md#cards)) would be needed;
  3. trim Blink at level 1 only.

  The owner's standing preference is 2 over 3.
- **Search quality** is recorded as a limit. The next search should seed from several
  starting hands per monster, as plan 34's Layer 4 describes.
