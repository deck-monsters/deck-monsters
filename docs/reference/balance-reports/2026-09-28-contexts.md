# Catalogue contexts, 2026-09-28

Roadmap 34 task 6b, from [Value beyond damage](../../roadmap/34-balance-methodology.md#value-beyond-damage-owner-2026-09-28).
The first [catalogue](2026-09-28-catalogue.md) put every card in a hand of Hits. Here every
card is also measured in four contexts, on the reference chassis at levels 3, 5, and 7:
- **caster:** an INT hand (Blast II, Brain Drain, Heal);
- **brute:** a STR and DEX hand (Hit Harder, Berserk, Hit);
- **drinks:** against an opponent that drinks and heals;
- **crowd:** a free-for-all with three opponents.

The contexts are defined in `balance/contexts.ts`. The plan is `plan:contexts` (defaults),
run with `sim:batch` and read with
`sim:contexts <run> 2026-09-28-catalogue.json`: 6,984 units, 352,692 fights, 0 failed, at
`a115ce5`. JSON: [2026-09-28-contexts.json](2026-09-28-contexts.json).

## How to read it

- **Duel contexts** (hits, caster, brute, drinks) are in Hit-equivalents for that context:
  `1 + (score − a Hit's score) / (a Hit's score − an empty slot's score)`. The card, the Hit,
  and the empty slot each sit once in every slot. **Hits** is the catalogue's sHE and
  **Field** its field value, both averaged over levels 3–7. The field column is shown but
  not ranked, since its baseline is small.
- **Crowd** is in **points over a Hit** in the same slot. In a four-way fight one Hit over an
  empty slot is worth only 2–3 points, so Hit-equivalents there would blow every value up.
- **Flags:**
  - *context card*: its best duel context is at least 0.7 over the Hit context.
  - *crowd card*: it gains 3 points or more over a Hit in a crowd.
  - *weak everywhere*: under 0.5 in every duel and no crowd gain. This is the only low value
    the method now treats as a finding.
  - *trap*: below an empty slot even in its best context. No card is one.

## Findings

1. **The acceptance check passes.**
   - Feline Companion reads 1.11 in the caster context (−0.25 in Hits).
   - Bad Batch reads 2.33 against an opponent that drinks (0.14 in Hits).
   - Both are context cards, as the owner said. Neither changes.
2. **The whole hide class is a crowd class.** In duels, Cloak of Invisibility, Camouflage Vest
   and Mood Scales are worth 0.1–0.4. In a crowd they gain 4–5 points over a Hit, and Take
   Wing gains 8. Hiding pays when others can be attacked instead. They are crowd cards, not
   weak ones.
3. **Heals are crowd cards too.**
   - Heal, Whiskey Shot and Scotch gain 10–14 points over a Hit in a crowd: survival decides a
     free-for-all.
   - In duels a single heal is worth about a Hit. The [search](2026-09-28-search.md) shows
     that several heals in one duel hand are worth much less.
4. **Area spells gain 11–14 points in a crowd**, as designed:
   - Sandstorm 14;
   - Blast and Blast II 12, Fire Breath 11;
   - Enchanted Faceswap 11.

   Sandstorm is also the strongest duel card (2.9 in the brute context).
5. **Weak everywhere**, in every duel and with no crowd gain:

   | Monster | Cards |
   |---|---|
   | Unicorn | Unconquerable Horn, Dissonant Voice, Gloaming Rest |
   | Weeping Angel | Mesmerize, Enthrall |
   | Dragon | Tsunami |
   | Any | Harden, Prion Disease |

   Under the owner's rule, a weak card needs a fix only when its monster has no competitive
   picks without it. The [search](2026-09-28-search.md) never kept these cards, and the
   Unicorn and Angel are in band without them. Tsunami matters because the Dragon is not in
   band. Prion Disease is a fun card.
6. **Boosts come to life in context.**
   - Ecdysis reads 1.32 against drinks and 1.02 in a brute hand, against 0.37 in Hits.
   - Basic Shield reads 0.68–0.76.
   - The search found boosts worth 20–37 points on real brutes at level 5.
