# Formula tables, 2026-09-28 (Layer 0)

**Run:** `sim:formula` at commit `803485a` (7 s, no fights). Each monster is built at each
level 200 times (its HP and AC vary per instance); a Hit's rolls come from the card's own
`hitCheck` and `rollForDamage`, 4,000 plays per cell against a random field opponent at the
same level. Turns to kill (TTK) is the field's mean HP over damage per play. JSON:
[2026-09-28-formula.json](2026-09-28-formula.json).

## Readings

- **The late-game hypothesis holds.** A Hit's TTK is flat around 6-9 plays through level
  10, then climbs to 13-18 at level 20. The Hit gets *worse* past level 10: AC keeps growing
  to level 12 while DEX stops at 10 and STR at 6, so the chance to land falls from about 70%
  to 60% and damage per play from about 7-8 to 6, while HP grows another 50%.
- **Blast's TTK falls instead** (8.8 at level 1 to about 5 from level 8), per opponent, and it
  hits every opponent without a roll. From level 3 it out-damages a Hit per target; at level
  20 it does three times a Hit's damage, to everyone.
- So late-game caster strength is structural before it is about any card: level-scaled
  spells track HP and flat strikes do not. Candidate fixes belong to the next pass (roadmap
  34, "Late-game strikes"), for example strikes that keep scaling past level 10 or a softer
  HP curve, measured against the whole matrix.
- The chassis differences are small and mostly early: the Minotaur and Basilisk hit
  hardest, the Weeping Angel's Hit is the weakest at every level (STR peaks at 5), and the
  Unicorn lands most often (DEX 12).
