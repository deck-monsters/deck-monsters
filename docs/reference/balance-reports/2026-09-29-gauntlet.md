# Gauntlet candidate fixes, 2026-09-29

Roadmap 38 balance study. It reports numbers for the owner to choose from; it decides nothing.
The engine switches are all off in play (`GAUNTLET_RULES.rivalsWhenAlone` and
`GAUNTLET_RULES.extrasAsMinions` in `ring/ring-events.ts`; `RING_EVENT_RULES.globalWeights`),
turned on only by the harness variants `gauntlet-rivals-alone`, `gauntlet-minions`,
`gauntlet-rivals-minions`, and `event-weights-global` (`balance/variants.ts`). Raw numbers:
[2026-09-29-gauntlet.json](2026-09-29-gauntlet.json).

## Method

- Command: `node dist/scripts/sim-gauntlet.js --variant <name|none|reference> --lone <levels> --pair <levels> --out <file>`
  (`packages/harness/src/scripts/sim-gauntlet.ts`), run once per variant, split into one process per
  level (a long process leaks about 2.5 GB per 12,000 fights).
- `SimConfig.forceRingEvent: 'gauntlet'` (harness only) activates the Gauntlet before every fight
  through the ring's own `activateRingEvent`, so its two extra bosses are spawned by the ring's own
  rules (level cap, budget, temperament). The reference column has no event.
- Lone human: each of the 7 monster types, a likely deck, levels 0, 1, 3, 5, 1,000 fights per type
  per level per variant (7,000 per level per column). The ring's own boss is one per human, its level
  drawn by the ring's rules as in `sim-rings.ts` (at most the human's level + 1, 35% at that ceiling,
  otherwise the human's level), its type random per batch of 20 fights.
- Two humans: levels 1 and 3, both teamless (so they unite as the Challengers), types random per
  batch, two ring bosses plus the Gauntlet's two, 3,000 fights per level per column.
- Seeds depend on the cell, not the variant, so columns share the boss draws. A number is the share
  of fights the human side won (a draw is not a win). Standard error in brackets.
- `gauntlet-rivals-alone` needs exactly one human, so with two it is identical to `gauntlet-now`
  (and `gauntlet-rivals-minions` to `gauntlet-minions`). The pair columns match exactly, which is the
  check that the switch does nothing there.

New rule (owner, second pass): `GAUNTLET_RULES.rivalsWhenOutnumbered`, decided at fight start, in any fight
except a mega boss's: when bosses (ambush minions counted as bosses) outnumber the non-boss contestants,
targeting drops teams as in Blood Feud. A lone human against a Gauntlet is 3 bosses vs 1 and two humans are
4 vs 2, so both become free-for-alls; one boss per human (1 vs 1, 2 vs 2) is unchanged. In the lone table
`rivals-outnumbered` equals `gauntlet-rivals-alone` and `rivals-outnumbered-minions` equals
`gauntlet-rivals-minions` to the last digit (same seeds, and 1 human + Gauntlet is always outnumbered), which
also shows that the rule fires exactly when it should. The pair columns are where they differ.
`rivals-outnumbered-minions` is `extrasAsMinions` with the outnumbered rule.

## Ambush: one human, one boss and one ambush minion (no event), human win %

The ambush (`BOSS_AMBUSH_CHANCE_PERCENT` = 10) is the chance that a timer-spawned boss arrives as one boss
beyond one per human, as a minion at a third of its HP. It needs a human already in the ring, so it can occur
in at most 10% of timer spawns; a ring that also has a Gauntlet is separate. The harness builds it as a boss
and a `minion: true` boss (`SimMonsterSpec.minion`), the ring's own re-application of the minion's HP at fight
start applies. The rule counts the minion as a boss (2 vs 1); for comparison, one boss and no minion is
59.1% at level 1 and 70.1% at level 3 (the reference column above).

## Frequency: chance per countdown that each event fires (today / event-weights-global, percent)

Arithmetic. `RING_EVENT_CHANCE_PERCENT` = 25. Total weight of all events = 30 + 20 + 20 + 15 + 15 = 100
(Gauntlet 30, Blood Feud 20, Common Cause 20, House War 15, Reckoning 15). Eligibility: Gauntlet needs
1+ human; Blood Feud 3+ contestants; Common Cause 2+ humans and 1+ boss; House War 3+ humans and no boss;
Reckoning 1+ boss and 2+ humans.
Today: `25 x weight / (sum of eligible weights)`. Global: `25 x weight / 100` if that event is eligible
(a pick landing on an ineligible event fires nothing).
Example, 1 human + 1 boss: only the Gauntlet is eligible (2 contestants), so today 25 x 30/30 = 25% and
global 25 x 30/100 = 7.5%. Example, 2 humans + 1 boss: eligible weights 30+20+20+15 = 85, so the Gauntlet is
25 x 30/85 = 8.82% today and 7.5% global, Blood Feud 25 x 20/85 = 5.88% vs 5%, Common Cause 5.88% vs 5%,
Reckoning 25 x 15/85 = 4.41% vs 3.75%; House War is 0 (a boss is present).

## Reading it

- Today a lone human loses nearly every Gauntlet (0.0 / 0.1 / 1.4 / 8.2% at levels 0 / 1 / 3 / 5) against
  45 / 59 / 70 / 72% for one boss.
- Rivals-alone recovers 25 / 36 / 60 / 71%; minions alone 1 / 4 / 18 / 32%; both give 48 / 57 / 69 / 74%,
  about the one-boss reference at every level.
- Two humans fare badly too (1.1% at level 1, 14.1% at level 3). The rivals switch does nothing there by
  design; minions lift them to 16.6 / 42.0%.
- Outnumbered-rivals gives two humans 35.9% (level 1) and 50.2% (level 3) against the Gauntlet, against 1.1 /
  14.1% today and 65.3 / 76.7% for two bosses with no event; with extras as minions too, 50.8 / 57.3%.
- The ambush minion, left alone, takes a lone human to 15.4% (level 1) and 34.9% (level 3); counted as a boss in
  the outnumbered rule it lifts them to 58.9 / 68.2%, about the one-boss reference (59.1 / 70.1%). So the
  rule also removes the ambush's punch: whether minions should count is the owner's call, and counting them
  makes the ambush a rare mild scare, not a scare at all.
- With global weights the Gauntlet fires 7.5% of countdowns on any roster, instead of 25% when it is the
  only eligible event (1 human, or 2 humans without a boss).

## Shipped mechanism (2026-09-30)

The owner shipped "rivals when outnumbered" with a different mechanism from the one measured above. That
study dropped teams for the whole fight, as Blood Feud does (`team: false`), so any two humans in it could hit
each other, even on the same Sorting Hat team. As shipped, when bosses (ambush minions included) outnumber the
humans at fight start, **each boss gets a team of its own for that fight** (a contestant-level `team`, the hook
ring events use); humans keep their real teams, teamless humans stay their own faction, and the Challengers
alliance is not formed. The rule skips a mega boss's fight, Blood Feud, the team events (Common Cause, House
War) and The Reckoning. It is on in play (`GAUNTLET_RULES.rivalsWhenOutnumbered`), as is
`RING_EVENT_RULES.globalWeights`. Raw numbers: [2026-09-30-gauntlet-shipped.json](2026-09-30-gauntlet-shipped.json).

Method as above with `--variant none` (the shipped rules), 1,000 fights per type (7,000 per lone level and per
ambush level), 3,000 fights per pair cell, one process per cell. The two-allied-humans cells use the new
`sim-gauntlet --pair-team <name>` flag (`SimMonsterSpec.sharedTeam`: a team on both humans without the
harness's last-team mode, so the Gauntlet still fires).

- The lone and teamless-pair numbers match the free-for-all study to within noise (24.8 / 36.1 / 59.6 / 70.6
  and 35.9 / 50.2 before): a lone human has no ally to spare, and two teamless humans still count as separate
  factions, so they can still hit each other. What the change buys is the team case: two humans who chose to
  share a team keep their alliance and win 67% and 83% at levels 1 and 3, above the two-bosses-no-event
  reference for two teamless humans (65% / 77%), where the free-for-all mechanism would have let them hit
  each other.
- The Gauntlet fired in every one of the forced fights (7,000 of 7,000 per lone level, 3,000 of 3,000 per pair
  cell), as the harness forces it.
- Proof the "before" variant works: `--variant no-rivals-outnumbered --lone 1 --fights 300` (7 types, 2,100
  fights) gives 1 win, 0.05%, against 36.3% shipped. An earlier version of `balance/variants.ts` wrote `true`
  to the switch, which was already `true`, so both "before" variants did nothing; they now write `false`.
