# Best-hand search and band check, 2026-09-28

Roadmap 34 task 9, lean cut. Run by the owner on `3c49abb` with
`sim-search --collection 2026-09-28-layer3-collection.json --workers 12` (defaults otherwise:
levels 1, 3, 5; 2 rounds; up to 10 steps; 16 moves a step; 20 fights a seat order; 100 per
matrix pair). Read with `sim-search-report`; JSON: [2026-09-28-search.json](2026-09-28-search.json).

## How to read it

- **Field average** is each monster's expected score (draws count half) against the other
  six searched hands, on seeds the search never used. The band is 35–75% (owner). "Below
  band" and "above band" are flagged only when the whole 95% interval is outside it.
- **Searched vs typical hand** is the searched hand against the hand the search started
  from. It measures how much good picks gain.
- **Two limits, both important:**
  1. **The search is underpowered.** Each candidate move got 240 fights (6 opponents × 2 seat
     orders × 20). At the 99% bar, only moves worth about 10 points or more could be kept.
     At levels 1 and 3 few moves were kept, so those hands are close to the typical starting
     hands, and better hands probably exist. Round 2 still changed 3 hands, so best response
     had not settled.
  2. **The starting hands over-held heals.** The collection model built typical hands by
     adding up single-card values, and a Heal is worth a little more than a Hit alone, so
     level-5 starting hands held 4 Heals and a Whiskey Shot. Heals do not add up that way
     (see below). So the level-5 "searched vs typical" column (84–96%) mostly measures how
     bad a five-heal hand is. It is not a measure of skill expression.

## Findings

1. **The Dragon is below or at the edge of the band at every primary level.** It sits at 23%
   at level 1 (21–26%), 34% at 3, and 33% at 5. The Weeping Angel beats it 95% at level 1.
   - At level 1 the search swapped **both Fire Breaths for Delayed Hits** (+6.7 and +11.5
     points, confirmed on fresh seeds). On the real Dragon against the real field, its
     signature card is worse than a Delayed Hit at level 1.
   - This is the clearest lead for the Dragon's early problem, together with its weak body
     and dead Tsunami ([Layer 3](2026-09-28-layer3.md)).
2. **The Gladiator is low at every primary level:** 37% at levels 1 and 3, and 32% at level 5
   (30–35%, below the band).
   - Its body is below par at every level ([Layer 3](2026-09-28-layer3.md)).
   - The Unicorn and Angel beat it hardest (as low as 17%).
   - This runs against the owner's goal of brutes being strong early.
3. **The Weeping Angel is the strongest at levels 1–5 (65–72%), and the Jinn at level 5
   (71%).**
   - Both field averages are in band, but the Angel has two matchups over 85%: 95% against
     the Dragon at level 1, and 93% against the Minotaur at level 5.
   - The Minotaur, in band at levels 1 and 3 (56–57%), falls to 36% at level 5, partly
     through that Angel matchup (7%).
4. **Level 3 is entirely in band** (Dragon 34% and Gladiator 37% at the edge).
5. **Heals do not stack.** At level 5, 14 of the 17 moves the search kept replaced a Heal or
   a Whiskey Shot with a strike, a boost, or control. One or two heals are worth about a Hit
   each, and more are worth much less. The collection model and any hand builder must
   stop valuing heals one at a time.
6. **Boosts are worth far more on real brutes in a real field than on the reference chassis.**
   - At level 5, Ecdysis on the Basilisk was worth +36.7 points in place of a Heal.
   - Adrenaline Rush on the Minotaur was worth +27.9, and on the Gladiator +20.2.
   - Together with the context results ([contexts](2026-09-28-contexts.md)), the boost
     class's low catalogue values were largely the Hit context's blind spot.

## Next steps

- **Fix candidates with robust evidence:** the Dragon (early, and its Fire Breath at level 1)
  and the Gladiator (every level). Both are low at every primary level with intervals clear
  of noise, and better search would lift other monsters' hands too.
- **Watch, not fix yet:** the Weeping Angel's over-85% matchups and the Jinn at level 5, until
  a stronger search confirms them.
- **A stronger confirmation run** (owner's machine, about an hour): more fights and moves,
  and starting hands with at most two heals. It will also be the "after" check for the
  first fixes.
