  - turnarounds from 50 behind: −2%.

## The level 1 Dragon against the Basilisk

This is the one matchup that crossed a line: 80.5% before, 85.2% after, just over the 85% cap.
A local rerun of that pair on the same seeds, with one variant at a time, puts all of the
move on the pin rule:

| Rule switched off | Dragon wins |
|---|---|
| None (after) | 85.2% |
| Pin rule | 80.5% |
| Take Wing's area dodge | 85.2% |
| Mesmerize's natural-1 self-catch | 85.2% |

- **This is the Faceswap ceiling.** The hand is the searched best level 1 Dragon, holding
  Enchanted Faceswap and Tail Lash, and it was already the matchup
  [11's Faceswap watch](../../roadmap/11-balance-and-mechanics.md) tracks.
- **Nobody plays it yet.** Production on 2026-09-29 had 3 Dragons, all level 0. None of the 116
  recorded fights listed Faceswap as a notable card.
- **The owner's call (2026-09-29): no hard change.** The watch stays open.
