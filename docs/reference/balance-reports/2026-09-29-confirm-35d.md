Each matrix is 2,000 fights a pair: about ±1 point on a field average and ±2 on a matchup.

**Part 1: the branch's code alone.** PR C's searched hands
([2026-09-28-search-35.json](2026-09-28-search-35.json)) were replayed on PR D's code and
compared with the owner's PR C run on the same hands and seeds.

**Part 2: the new cards in play.** A new `sim-search` at levels 1, 3, 5, and 7 used
[2026-09-29-collection-35d.json](2026-09-29-collection-35d.json). That collection is the Layer 3
collection with the Dragon and Unicorn also owning their new and reworked cards. Its matrix is
compared with part 1, so both sides run the same code and the difference is the hands.

## Part 1: the code changes moved almost nothing on the old hands

No field average moved more than 1.3 points at any level, and the excitement guardrails were
flat: rounds 0%, Curse of Loki 0%, strokes of luck 0%, turnarounds −1% and +1%. PR C's hands
hold none of the reworked or new cards; only Faceswap appears, in the level 7 Jinn and Weeping
Angel hands, and it did not change. So part 1 shows that PR D's shared changes disturb nothing
else, including the ward consumers, the runner, and the card plumbing. What the reworked cards
are worth is measured in the studies and in part 2.

## Part 2: the Dragon gains real options

| Level | Dragon before → after | Unicorn before → after | Field averages after | Flags after |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 28% → **63%** | 61% → 43% | 39–67% | Unicorn vs Dragon 16%, Basilisk vs Dragon 20% (under 20%) |
| 3 | 46% → **63%** | 50% → 51% | 40–63% | none |
| 5 | 51% → 59% | 49% → 47% | 36–66% | Weeping Angel vs Minotaur 85% (Minotaur 15%) |
| 7 | 42% → 38% | 58% → 57% | 38–67% | Unicorn vs Dragon 97% (Dragon 3%), pre-existing |

Every field average is inside the 35–75% band at every level.

**What the search picked:**

- **Dragon.**
  - Level 1: Enchanted Faceswap (+13.2 as a move), Tail Lash (+11.0), and a reorder (+9.8).
  - Level 3: Faceswap (+6.7) and Lucky Strike (+9.8).
  - Level 5: Faceswap (+5.7) and a reorder.
  - Level 7: kept its typical hand. No move held.
  - It never chose Helm of Awe or Asinine Companion. They measured level with the card they
    replace, so they are real choices that a search for the strongest hand passes over.
- **Unicorn.** It chose none of its reworked cards at any level. That fits the studies: they are
  now at par or close, usable and not dominant. The owner asked for exactly that: useful and
  interesting cards, not a stronger Unicorn.

**The level 1 swing is a ceiling, not the typical player.** Faceswap is rare and not for sale.
The collection gives every level 1 Dragon one copy, which few real players have. So 63% is what
a Dragon owner who builds around the new cards can reach. The level 1 matrix is also fairer than
before:

- **Before:** field averages spread from 28% to 69%, and the worst matchups were Dragon 7% and
  Weeping Angel 93% vs the Dragon.
- **After:** field averages spread from 39% to 67%, and the worst matchups are the Unicorn at 16%
  and the Dragon at 84% in the same matchup.

The Unicorn's level 1 drop (61% to 43%) is almost all that one matchup.

## Open items this run surfaced

These are recorded in
[11's next balance pass](../../roadmap/11-balance-and-mechanics.md#next-balance-pass-carried-from-roadmap-35).

- **The level 7 Dragon against the Unicorn (3%)** was 2% in PR C's run too. A diagnosis on the
  searched hands (300 fights each) found the cause is the Unicorn's four Blasts:
  - Even a Dragon holding nine Hits wins only 8%.
  - Replacing the Unicorn's Blasts with Hits lifts the Dragon to 26%.
  - Removing the Dragon's Take Wings lifts it only to 9%.
  - Blast needs no attack roll, and something in the Dragon's level 7 body makes it fold.
    Other Blast users, such as the Weeping Angel, beat it only 62–67%.
- **The level 1 Dragon with Faceswap** against the Unicorn (84%) and the Basilisk (80%) is under
  the 85% cap but outside the 20–80% target. Watch it as real players collect Faceswap before
  trimming anything.
- **The level 5 Weeping Angel against the Minotaur (85%)** rose as the search found Blink for
  the Angel. The owner's decision stands: Blink stays, and counters come first (the counter
  cards in [12](../../roadmap/12-new-content-backlog.md#cards)).
